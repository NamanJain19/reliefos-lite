Data Provenance & Verification Protocol

ISO-19115 GIS Standard

To maintain zero-confusion operational reliability during flash inundation emergencies in Kamrup Metropolitan, every upstream attribute fed into the allocation solvers is tagged with one of six provenance classes:

1. OBSERVED sensors

Direct physical gauge or sensor telemetry (e.g., CWC Pandu stage reading).

2. FORECAST cloud

Numerical weather prediction runs & projected rainfall accumulation (IMD).

3. ESTIMATED insights

Algorithmic derivations, SAR surface thresholding, and zonal demand models.

4. MANUAL how_to_reg

Disaster officer certified field audits, bridge closure reports & depot logs.

5. SIMULATED model_training

Synthetic stress-test configurations and artificial dam-release scenarios.

6. STALE / FALLBACK history

Telemetry exceeding safe freshness TTL, actively substituted with certified baselines.
